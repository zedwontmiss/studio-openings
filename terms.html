Employer posting terms

Effective 20 November 2026

What is offered

Standard costs USD 149 for up to 30 days of a reviewed job listing. Featured costs USD 349 for up to 90 days, with rotating highlighted placement when eligible. Both are one-time fees for one verified vacancy. No impressions, applications, candidates, interviews or hires are guaranteed.

Review and payment

Submitting a request is free and does not publish a vacancy. Studio Openings may reject inaccurate, unverifiable, unrelated, misleading or unlawful postings. Accepted employers receive a manual invoice. Publication only follows confirmation of payment through an authorized commercial payment method and final vacancy review. Please do not pay until you receive verified instructions directly from Studio Openings.

Timing and continued accuracy

Purchased periods begin upon publication, not submission or invoicing. Listings may be removed before their maximum term if the job closes, the employer requests removal, or the listing cannot be verified or breaches our editorial standards. Highlighted placement rotates and varies with filters.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

Before payment, employers may withdraw a request without charge. If you wish to cancel after paying, or believe an advertised service was not delivered as agreed, contact us with your order reference. Refund eligibility is reviewed based on the facts, service already delivered, payment-provider rules, and applicable consumer or contract law; these terms do not override mandatory legal rights. Do not assume an automatic full refund after publication.

Employer responsibilities

The submitting employer confirms they are authorized to advertise the vacancy and that the role, location, eligibility, compensation where supplied, and application route are truthful. Employers should notify us promptly of changes or closure. Candidates apply subject to the original employer's requirements.

Contact and privacy

Questions, corrections, invoices, or disputes: hello@s-o.app. Personal data handling is described in our Privacy page. These terms describe our paid advertising service and are not a tax invoice or a representation of an employer partnership for independently indexed jobs.
