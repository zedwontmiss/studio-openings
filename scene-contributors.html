S/O

Founding contributors

Help shape an independent local noticeboard for creative work and culture. We are exploring London and New York as initial pilots.

What you can contribute

Share local creative knowledge, suggest public meetups and exhibitions, introduce us to community groups, and advise on what makes the local Scene useful.

How it works

Voluntary collaboration, no posting quota, no required public profile and no unpaid employment commitment. You can stop participating at any time. Contributors do not receive access to private submissions or moderation credentials. Any paid work would be agreed separately.

Express interest

Email hello@s-o.app ↗ with your city, creative interests and what you would like to contribute. No CV or sensitive information needed. Please read our community guidelines and privacy information.
