# Studio acquisition and relationship qualification

Owner direction: build financial or social capital through discovery of independent creative practices, primarily established less than ten years ago. Preserve existing geographic tiers and multidisciplinary scope. Directory inclusion and editorial independence are not conditional on payment.

## Discovery and evidence
- Verify official identity, location, portfolio, founding date (year alone is insufficient at the ten-year boundary), and operating status before publication.
- Use direct discovery, graduate exhibitions, festivals, editorial coverage and collaborator credits.
- Exclude ambiguous evidence until resolved. Do not infer founder age, headcount, hiring, purchasing intent or financial condition.
- Record official evidence URL and review date for each material claim.
- Recheck existing records before outreach; technical CI does not establish factual accuracy.

## Relationship qualification
- Commercial: verified current recruitment, documented team growth, commercial client work, funded development, or a demonstrable sponsorship fit. A careers page alone is not an active vacancy.
- Social: documented creative practice and a concrete editorial, community or collaboration fit. Interest in participation is unknown until confirmed.
- Both: independent evidence of commercial and social fit.
- Unqualified: insufficient evidence; hold for review rather than inflate the pipeline.
- Prioritize active verified hiring first for sales outreach; established client work without hiring is a nurture lead.
- Keep actual outreach, contact details, scores and correspondence in a private access-controlled system, never the static site or public repository. Do not send outreach without owner authorization.

## Measures
Track indexed studios separately from qualified commercial prospects, prospective community relationships, replies, confirmed contributors and paid conversions. No automatic publication or outreach from this policy.
