## Outcome definition

A verified employer job should get a canonical crawlable page with accurate non-invented data, conditionally valid `JobPosting` structured data and removal on expiry. A sourced job must not be promoted to SEO until current availability, geography, source permission and content completeness are verified. A non-technical owner should not need to review routine code commits.
