## Owner-approved direction
Three principal products: jobs (recruitment), studio (permanent company index), article (independent editorial publication inspired by Medium/Substack). Flow: verified studio index by location -> original city/discipline articles -> independently verified jobs and optional paid employer posting. Editorial and advertising must remain separate. Directory rows open an internal profile; official external links appear within the profile. Single stable canonical studio identity even for multi-office companies.

Prioritize US and Canada for initial city sourcing. Other markets remain eligible under existing coverage policy; a reputable verified studio outside a major metro is not excluded. Build verified location records and evidence, not invented inventory. Public business facts may be independently indexed where lawful; no indiscriminate copying, restricted scraping, personal contact harvesting, logos/photos without rights, or implied endorsement. Record source URL, review date, location evidence, corrections/removal path. Employer verification distinct from editorial verification and never sold as credibility. Legal review before scaling collection or outreach across jurisdictions. Email is not a blanket prerequisite for public facts; commercial outreach requires jurisdiction-specific compliance, opt-out and existing sourcing holds.
