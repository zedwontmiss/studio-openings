# Studio project intelligence

Project records use stable studio IDs and may credit more than one practice. The five output types are canonical-project, owned-platform, original-product, research and cultural-initiative. Distinguish ownership, creation, collaboration and commissioned work; never assume ownership from a portfolio appearance.

The public-fact candidate registry is studio-registry/projects.json. Candidate status is not verified publication, and the pilot is not rendered or added to the sitemap. Reviewers must independently inspect the official source, confirm attribution, date if documented, and factual claims before changing status to reviewed. Independent evidence may supplement official evidence. Original summaries only; no copied project descriptions or unlicensed imagery. No project rankings, sales scoring, personal contacts or private outreach data in this registry.

Validation tests check schema, stable studio references, unique project URLs, evidence dates, attribution and explicit reviewed status. They do not fetch external sources or prove factual accuracy. Editorial publication remains a separate reviewed workflow. A project may inform Article or SIGNAL only after review; no automatic article generation or public ranking.
