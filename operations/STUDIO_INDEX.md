# S/O Index v1.0
Internal, evidence-based, cross-disciplinary research. Not a ranking of quality, prestige or employment suitability. No public score, commercial or social inputs, paid advantage, follower counts, founder age, or inferred financial performance.

Relevance: originality 40%, distinctive body of work 35%, cultural contribution 25%.
Momentum: recent original output 40%, self-initiated work 35%, creative exchange 25%. Evidence events must be dated within the previous 365 days. Missing evidence is unknown, not zero. Each criterion uses 0 (documented absence), 1 (limited), 2 (clear), 3 (strong), 4 (exceptional). Strong/exceptional requires official and independently authored corroboration from different domains. Review the actual sources; the software validates format, not truth or independence. Do not use copied press releases as independent evidence.

No group score until all three criteria have evidence. Score is weighted level divided by four. Reviews older than 180 days are stale. Compare within discipline and practice context; rubric weights are provisional and require calibration against independently reviewed cases. Do not equate publication visibility with merit. No cross-discipline leaderboard or public ranking authorized.

The authenticated isolated Preview endpoint stores separate JSON records with optimistic revision checks. Explicit initialization required. No records seeded, no D1 changes performed by deployment. Workshop is preview-only and noindex; page shell contains no private records. Enter operator secret separately, held only in tab memory. Evidence entry is manual JSON in this pilot. No automated scraping, public output, or outreach. Commercial and social assessments remain separate.
