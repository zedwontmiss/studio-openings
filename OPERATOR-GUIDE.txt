STUDIO OPENINGS v0.12 — PREMIUM INVENTORY PILOT
=============================================

1. WHAT CHANGED
- Ads stay inside the existing approved-v0.11.1 baby-yellow listing design.
- Maximum of 10 yellow sponsored jobs in a filtered feed (not 10 per scroll/page).
- Limit is further adjusted to about 1 sponsored job per 5 visible results.
- In Best Match, the selected paid jobs are spread across ordinary jobs.
- The sponsored campaign selection changes once per UTC day, predictably.
- Explicit sorts (date, company, A-Z) preserve their normal ordering.
- All existing location/category/job type filters remain effective.
- Campaigns can last up to 90 calendar days, and expire automatically.
- A job that closes ceases to be featured; it should also be removed from the board by the existing source pipeline.

2. WHAT HAS NOT CHANGED
- This release does not add payments, checkout, bidding, an admin panel, pricing,
  or a promise of a specific number of impressions.
- Ordinary job submissions and the featured request checkbox stay unchanged.
- No paid campaigns are activated in assets/featured-jobs.json (empty by default).
- The existing Cloudflare/D1 setup is not changed.

3. IMPORTANT SALES POLICY
- Only a limited number of yellow placements is *visible* to each visitor.
- If there are more than 10 active campaigns, they rotate; a paid advertiser may not
  appear yellow in every feed, filtered view or session. Disclose this to employers.
- Daily rotation alone cannot promise equal impressions or applicants. Before selling,
  measure traffic and publish honest terms about rotation, filters and expiration.
- Do NOT take money yet. Payment is not connected or verified.

4. OWNER ACTIVATION (FUTURE; CURRENTLY EMPTY)
- Review real featured requests privately in Cloudflare D1.
- Approve the employer and verify their job is an eligible direct- or firm- listing.
- Verify a real payment separately when a payment provider is eventually connected.
- Only then enter an existing live jobId into assets/featured-jobs.json as below.
- Never enter user email, payment details, private data, or invented job IDs.

Sample entry for documentation only — DO NOT copy into production:
{
  "version": 1,
  "placements": [
    {"jobId":"direct-so-SAMPLE123", "startsOn":"2027-01-01", "endsOn":"2027-03-31", "approved":true}
  ]
}
