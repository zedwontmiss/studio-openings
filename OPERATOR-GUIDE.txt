STUDIO OPENINGS — V1.0 OPERATOR GUIDE (MANUAL INVOICE)
===================================================

NO PROVIDER CHECKOUT IS CONNECTED. NO EMAIL IS SENT BY THIS SOFTWARE.
Only you may authorize editorial review and attest to payment. Do not share the secret.

PRICING
  Standard: USD 149 for up to 30 days of public visibility
  Featured: USD 349 for up to 90 days, rotating baby-yellow/stared listing
  Spotlight: NOT SOLD — unimplemented
  Visitor vacancy suggestion: free editorial source, not an advertising service

WORKFLOW / OPERATOR CONSOLE
  1. Unlock /operator.html with Cloudflare OPERATOR_ACCESS_TOKEN Secret.
  2. Requests -> pending_review: verify the employer, role, location, source and applicant route. Press Review passed.
  3. ready_to_invoice: use Copy invoice email, prepare an actual invoice under your legal seller identity (amount and currency) and send it from your verified hello@s-o.app mailbox. Clicking Copy does NOT send.
  4. ONLY after the actual invoice has gone out, click Invoice actually sent.
  5. awaiting_payment: confirm bank/provider funds received for the correct order amount. Record a private payment reference. NEVER accept an employer screenshot as proof.
  6. paid: recheck vacancy; click Publish paid job. Standard goes live up to 30 days. Featured goes live up to 90 days only when PREMIUM_ACTIVATION_ENABLED=true.
  7. Send a separate manual paid/published confirmation email to the employer using the Copy email buttons. The site does not send email automatically.
  8. Recheck each published vacancy at least every 30 days. Job freshness is intentionally capped. Purchased duration is NOT reset when reverified.

WARNINGS
  - Paid posts cannot be rejected with the simple Reject button; resolve refunds/disputes manually first. No automated refund feature.
  - Featured rows have at most ten visible placements in a filtered feed, with a capacity limit and rotation. Never guarantee impression counts or number of applicants.
  - An order paid for 90 days may disappear early if vacancy closes, fails verification, or no longer qualifies. Tell buyers about those conditions.
  - Verify real bank transfer acceptance for overseas buyers and actual sender account BEFORE inviting paid employers.
  - Store no API keys, operator passwords, banking details or customer payment identifiers in GitHub source.
  - Never use real payment attestations to simulate QA. Local automated tests use isolated temporary SQLite.

MIGRATION
  Preview D1 first: OWNER-SETUP/001_job_orders.sql (single SQL statement).
  Confirm via /tables -> job_orders visible. Do not run on production until the v1.0 launch is approved.

SCOPE
  Payments: manual only; applications: external link or email preferred; native application form disabled.
  Newsletter, bulk email, credit cards, one-click checkout, and Spotlight not yet implemented.
