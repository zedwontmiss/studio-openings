Community guidelines

Scene is a place for creative work, local culture and constructive conversation. Participation does not require a public name or profile.

Open discussion, including criticism

Meetups, salary comparisons, workplace criticism and candid personal opinions are welcome. Negative opinions are not by themselves grounds for removal. Clearly distinguish firsthand experience, opinion and verifiable factual claims. Do not share confidential or identifying information about colleagues.

Discuss ideas, not personal identities

Do not publish private contact details, personal information, threats, harassment, discriminatory abuse or unsupported allegations. Share your own experiences without identifying private individuals. Do not impersonate others or fabricate evidence.

Keep it relevant

Discuss creative work and local culture. No spam, deceptive promotion, illegal content or unsolicited recruitment advertising. Public meetups should state the date, local time and a public venue; avoid private residential addresses. Do not assume attendance or safety is verified by SOAPP. Commercial placements belong on the advertise page.

Moderation and reporting

Submissions are reviewed before publication. Moderators may reject or remove content. Use the report control or contact Studio Openings for safety, privacy or removal requests. Never include sensitive information in a public post.

Privacy

Anonymous means no public profile, not guaranteed technical anonymity. Verification and abuse prevention may process technical information. See privacy and contact. Scene is a restricted development pilot; these guidelines are subject to review before public launch.
