Practice

Your architecture portfolio should answer the job brief.

A practical approach to tailoring project evidence for concept, technical and BIM-focused architecture roles.

ArchitecturePortfolio
S/O
Studio OpeningsNov 24, 2026 · 1 min read
Share by email

Identify what the practice needs

An architectural designer, technical architect and BIM coordinator may work on the same project but provide different evidence of competence. Read the role's responsibilities before selecting portfolio pages. Separate software familiarity from experience delivering a specific project stage.

Select projects by contribution

For each project, explain the brief, your responsibilities, the team context and the decisions you contributed to. A concept role might benefit from diagrams and iterations. A documentation role might benefit from coordinated drawings and detail development. Show only work you have permission to share.

Make the evidence easy to inspect

Use legible drawings, captions, consistent scales where relevant and a short explanation of the problem solved. Avoid an entire portfolio of polished renders if the vacancy asks for technical coordination. A short, relevant selection can communicate more than an unedited archive.

Check the application instructions

Practices may request a file size limit, a particular format, a cover letter or a professional registration. Confirm these requirements on the original vacancy. Never claim a qualification or individual responsibility you did not hold.
