London: where design studios meet cultural institutions

A selective starting point for finding brand, digital design, architecture, motion and exhibition employers in London.

S/O
Studio OpeningsNov 25, 2026 · 2 min read
Share by email

A creative career in London does not have to follow a single discipline. Our directory brings together independent design practices, international production teams and cultural institutions. This is a curated starting point, not a ranking or a comprehensive map of London's creative economy.

Brand and visual identity

Pentagram, DixonBaxi, Ragged Edge, Further and Design Bridge and Partners offer different starting points for exploring brand and visual design. Examine their published projects and look for the balance between strategy, identity systems and production. A portfolio for a branding role should make the underlying design decisions as clear as the finished work.

Digital products and moving images

ustwo is a digital product design entry in our directory; Framestore represents motion and visual effects. These fields often ask for different evidence: product designers can explain interactions and constraints, while motion applicants can show timing, storytelling and their contribution to a reel.

Architecture and exhibition work

Adjaye Associates represents architecture in this selection. Tate, the Victoria and Albert Museum and the Design Museum provide another route into creative work through cultural institutions. Look beyond job titles such as designer: exhibition production, interpretation, digital experiences and technical coordination may also be relevant.

How to use this guide

This selection is based on the London location records in the Studio Openings directory as of 25 November 2026. Follow each profile to its official website or career source. An entry does not establish a current vacancy, a partnership, a salary level or the size of its London office. Check the employer directly before applying.
