Privacy & contact

Studio Openings is a creative-job directory and, when enabled, a place for employers to post openings and candidates to apply. This page explains the submission features we operate.

Employer submissions

If you submit a job, we collect the fields you enter, including your contact email. Job submissions are stored privately for manual review. Only approved job information is published. Your private contact email is not included in public job-listing data unless you specifically choose it as the application email.

Applications on Studio Openings

When native applications are enabled, we collect your name and email address, plus optional portfolio URL and introduction, and the vacancy you applied to. Applications are stored privately for manual review and forwarding to the relevant verified hiring organization. They are not delivered instantly, and we do not provide employers with public access to the application database.

Other application methods

Jobs may link to an external hiring website or use an application email address. Those external services and employers operate under their own privacy practices. Studio Openings does not receive the contents of applications submitted elsewhere through those links.

Directory traffic

When enabled, we count page views, visits and clicks on Apply by day and broad referral source (such as Reddit, X or search). A click records interest in a vacancy. A tab remembers only its referral category for the visit. The aggregate table stores counts and directory job IDs. It contains no cookies, personal visitor identifiers, IP addresses, full referrers or query strings. We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. On collection, we discard daily totals older than 90 days.

Document imports

Text extraction from PDF, DOCX or text files runs in your browser. The original uploaded file is not sent to our job-submission API. Only the structured details you review and choose to submit are sent.

Requests and retention

You may request access to or deletion of information you submitted, or ask about an application, using the contact below. We process these requests manually. Automatic expiry and deletion of stored candidate applications have not yet been implemented; we do not claim automatic erasure.

Contact: Contact address not configured. Native submissions are not available until the operator configures this information.

Security

We validate requests using Cloudflare Turnstile and process records with Cloudflare Pages Functions and D1. We do not offer a public endpoint to read candidate application data. No website or storage service can guarantee absolute security.

Last updated: 22 November 2026. Please review this text before enabling native applications, especially applicable privacy-law requirements in your target countries.
